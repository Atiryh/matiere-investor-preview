# Matiere

**AI creative direction for coherent product campaigns**

Atelier Concepts — Matiere is an early-stage product for developing campaign concepts from a product image and a creative brief. It is being built to help teams explore visual directions, keep the product and brand consistent, and turn a chosen direction into a storyboard and production workflow.

## The problem

AI tools can generate individual images. Building a whole campaign that stays faithful to the same product, brief, and brand is harder. Teams need creative control across concepts, frames, and formats.

## The approach

The Matiere workflow is designed around five steps:

1. Start with a product image and its defining details.
2. Add the audience, campaign goal, and creative brief.
3. Explore distinct campaign directions.
4. Build a storyboard with chosen visual roles and formats.
5. Generate, review, and package the approved assets.

The current public examples focus on fashion. The workflow is intended to support other product categories, including beauty, jewelry, and ecommerce.

## Stage and next steps

The public site shows the product vision and example campaign directions. Matiere is in demo and product-development stage. Current priorities are product-identity consistency, controllable storyboards, visual quality review, and a repeatable photo and video workflow.
